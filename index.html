References, assumptions and limitations

The formulas are simplified single-degree-of-freedom teaching models. Parameter ranges are adjustable demonstration ranges, not certified machine or structural design limits.

  • ISO 20816-1:2026 — general principles for measuring and evaluating machine vibration.
  • ISO 10137:2007 — vibration serviceability of buildings and walkways.
  • SKF Introduction Guide to Vibration Monitoring — vibration quantities and condition-monitoring concepts.
  • Bosch Rexroth technical information: Bellow actuators — industrial vibration-isolation applications and the need to check force/stroke data for the selected component.
  • WEG W22 motor product data — example of a 4-pole, 50 Hz motor with 1500 rpm synchronous speed (actual rated speed is slightly lower).

This application is for learning and preliminary exploration only. It is not a substitute for site measurements, manufacturer data for the exact machine, a qualified engineer, or the full requirements of applicable standards. Do not use its warning colours as ISO acceptance zones.
